The federal funds rate is one of the most closely watched numbers in finance. It shapes borrowing costs, valuations, capital investment, IPO activity and currency markets across every sector of the U.S. economy. The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) sets the target range for this rate, and its next decision is due at the upcoming December 8–9 meeting.
